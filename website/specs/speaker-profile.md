# Speaker Profile Page Specification

## Purpose

The Speaker Profile page helps organizations evaluate whether a specific speaker is the right fit for their audience, goals and conversation context.

This page is a key trust-building and conversion page.

It should balance emotional connection, credibility and practical booking information.

---

## Primary Goals

1. Build trust in the speaker
2. Communicate the speaker’s lived experience and perspective
3. Help organizations assess fit
4. Drive booking requests

Primary action:

- Request a Session

Secondary actions:

- Browse More Speakers
- Book a Speaker

---



## User Questions

The Speaker Profile page should answer:

- Who is this speaker?
- What is their lived experience or story focus?
- What topics do they speak about?
- What formats are available?
- Is this the right speaker for my audience?
- How do I request a session?

---



## Page Structure

1. Hero / Introduction
2. Speaker Story
3. Topics & Expertise
4. Session Formats
5. Testimonials / Social Proof
6. Booking CTA
7. Related Speakers (optional)
8. Footer

---



# Sections



## 1. Hero / Introduction



### Purpose

Introduce the speaker with immediate clarity and emotional connection.

This section should quickly communicate:

- who they are
- what they bring
- why their perspective matters

---



### Content

Includes:

- Speaker photo
- Name
- Role / descriptor
- Short introduction
- Primary CTA

---



### Actions

Primary CTA:

- Request a Session

Secondary CTA:

- Browse Speakers

---



## 2. Speaker Story



### Purpose

Provide meaningful context around the speaker’s lived experience.

The goal is not to tell every detail, but to create trust and help users understand perspective.

---



### Content

May include:

- lived experience summary
- personal background
- perspective areas
- motivation for speaking

---



### UX Notes

This section should feel:

- authentic
- human
- respectful

Avoid turning personal experience into overly polished marketing language.

---



## 3. Topics & Expertise



### Purpose

Clarify what the speaker can talk about.

This helps organizations assess relevance and fit.

---



### Content

Examples:

- LGBTQ+ inclusion
- identity and belonging
- workplace allyship
- leadership and inclusion
- personal storytelling

---



## 4. Session Formats



### Purpose

Help users understand how the speaker can engage with audiences.

---



### Format Examples

- Keynote
- Fireside Chat
- Panel
- Workshop
- Q&A Session

---



### Additional Details

May include:

- preferred audience size
- session duration
- language availability
- remote / in-person availability

---



## 5. Testimonials / Social Proof



### Purpose

Increase credibility and trust.

This section helps reduce uncertainty before booking.

---



### Content

May include:

- testimonials
- feedback quotes
- previous session highlights

---



## 6. Booking CTA



### Purpose

Convert interest into action.

This section should feel clear and low-friction.

---



### Messaging Goal

Communicate:

- booking is simple
- requests are reviewed carefully
- speaker fit matters

---



### Actions

Primary CTA:

- Request a Session

---



## 7. Related Speakers (Optional)



### Purpose

Help users continue exploration if this speaker is not the right fit.

---



### Actions

- View Another Speaker
- Browse Speakers

---



# Interaction Notes



## Scroll Experience

The page should feel calm, premium and immersive.

Scrolling should support:

- emotional connection
- credibility
- conversion

The experience should feel more editorial than transactional.

---



## CTA Behavior

The Request Session CTA should remain easy to access throughout the page.

Options:

- sticky CTA
- repeated CTA sections
- persistent booking button

---



## Motion

Subtle motion can reinforce the premium experience.

Examples:

- smooth section transitions
- image parallax
- fade-in reveals

Motion should support trust and focus.

---



# Success Metrics

The Speaker Profile page succeeds when users:

- spend meaningful time on the page
- understand the speaker quickly
- trust the speaker and platform
- move toward booking
