# Speakers Page Specification

## Purpose

The Speakers page helps organizations discover, browse and evaluate speakers based on story, expertise and session fit.

Its purpose is to transform general interest into speaker exploration and profile visits.

This page should feel curated, human and easy to navigate.

It should not feel like a generic speaker marketplace or talent directory.

---

## Primary Goals

1. Help users discover speakers
2. Support browsing and filtering
3. Build trust through strong speaker previews
4. Encourage profile exploration

Primary actions:

- View Profile
- Book a Speaker

---



## User Questions

The Speakers page should answer:

- Who are the available speakers?
- What stories and perspectives do they bring?
- What topics do they speak about?
- Who is the best fit for my audience or organization?

---



## Page Structure

1. Page Intro
2. Search and Filters
3. Speaker Listing
4. CTA / Footer

---



# Sections



## 1. Page Intro



### Purpose

Introduce the speaker network and set expectations.

This section should frame the speakers as real people sharing lived experiences, not as static profiles.

---



### Messaging Goal

Communicate:

- diversity of perspectives
- human stories
- thoughtful conversations

---



## 2. Search and Filters



### Purpose

Help users narrow down relevant speakers.

Filters should feel useful without overwhelming the experience.

---



### Recommended Filters

- Topic
- Identity / Perspective
- Language
- Session Format
- Availability (optional)

---



### Search

Users should be able to search by:

- speaker name
- topic
- keyword

---



## 3. Speaker Listing



### Purpose

Provide an easy-to-scan overview of available speakers.

The experience should feel curated and editorial.

---



### Speaker Card Content

Each card may include:

- Speaker photo
- Name
- Role or descriptor
- Story focus
- Topics
- Languages
- Session formats

---



### Card Behavior

Cards should feel interactive and premium.

Recommended interactions:

- hover animations
- subtle motion
- smooth transitions

Cards should encourage curiosity and profile exploration.

---



### Primary CTA

- View Profile

Secondary CTA:

- Book a Speaker

---



## 4. Footer / Final CTA



### Purpose

Support next steps for users who have not found the right fit.

---



# Interaction Notes



## Browsing Experience

The browsing experience should feel:

- simple
- fast
- curated
- visually clean

Users should be able to move easily between:

- list
- profile
- booking

---



## Filtering Behavior

Filtering should feel immediate and intuitive.

Results should update dynamically when possible.

---



## Motion

Subtle motion can enhance the premium feel.

Examples:

- hover effects
- image movement
- fade transitions

Motion should remain lightweight and refined.

---



# Success Metrics

The Speakers page succeeds when users:

- browse multiple speakers
- use filters effectively
- visit speaker profiles
- continue toward booking

---



# Open Questions

- Should speaker cards prioritize story or expertise?
- Should filters be minimal or more advanced?
- Should the page feel more editorial or more directory-based?
