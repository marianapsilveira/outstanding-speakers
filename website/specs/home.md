# Home Page Specification

## Purpose

The Home page is the main entry point for most users.

Its purpose is to:

- introduce OUTstanding Speakers
- communicate why meaningful conversations matter
- build trust and credibility
- guide users toward speaker exploration or booking

The page should feel human, modern and emotionally engaging without losing clarity.

---

## Primary Goals

1. Explain the value of the initiative
2. Build emotional and strategic relevance
3. Increase trust in the speaker network
4. Drive users toward action

Primary actions:

- Explore Speakers
- Book a Speaker

Secondary actions:

- Browse Resources
- Become a Speaker

---



## User Questions

The Home page should answer:

- What is OUTstanding Speakers?
- Why does this matter?
- Why should I trust this?
- How does it work?
- What can I do next?

---



## Page Structure

# Interaction Notes



## Scroll Behavior

The Home page should feel cinematic and immersive.

Scrolling should create a sense of layered movement and narrative progression rather than simply revealing stacked sections.

The experience should feel:

- smooth
- premium
- intentional
- editorial

---



### Parallax Behavior

Subtle parallax effects should be used to create depth across key sections.

Recommended behavior:

- background videos move slower than foreground content
- large typography may shift slightly during scroll
- image layers can move at different speeds
- section transitions should feel fluid and natural

Parallax should enhance storytelling without becoming distracting.

Avoid:

- aggressive motion
- excessive movement
- gimmicky animation

---



### Section Transitions

Transitions between sections should feel soft and seamless.

Examples:

- fade in on scroll
- subtle upward motion
- layered reveals
- smooth opacity transitions

The transition rhythm should support the narrative flow:

emotion → reflection → trust → action

---



### Performance Considerations

Motion effects should remain lightweight and performant.

Important:

- smooth on desktop
- stable on mobile
- no noticeable lag
- accessible for reduced-motion users

Parallax effects should degrade gracefully on lower-performance devices.

---



## CTA Behavior

CTAs should remain consistent throughout the page.

Avoid too many competing actions.

---



## Visual Behavior

Visual hierarchy should prioritize:

1. Messaging
2. Speaker credibility
3. Conversion

Background videos and imagery should support the narrative, not distract from it.

---
