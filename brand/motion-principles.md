# Motion Principles

## Purpose

Motion is part of the OUTstanding Speakers brand experience.

It should make the website feel immersive, editorial and alive, while keeping the experience calm and respectful.

Motion should support storytelling, not distract from it.

---

## Motion Personality

Motion should feel:

- cinematic
- subtle
- smooth
- atmospheric
- premium
- intentional

Avoid motion that feels:

- playful
- gimmicky
- fast
- noisy
- decorative without purpose

---

## Core Motion Behaviors

### 1. Slow Parallax

Use subtle parallax to create depth across key sections.

Best used for:

- hero background videos
- large editorial sections
- abstract visual layers

Behavior:

- background moves slower than foreground
- text remains readable and stable
- movement should be slow and controlled

---



### 2. Soft Reveals

Use fade-in and slight upward movement when content enters the viewport.

Best used for:

- speaker cards
- resource cards
- section headings
- CTA blocks

---



### 3. Ambient Background Movement

Video or abstract backgrounds may use slow movement to create atmosphere.

This should feel cinematic, not distracting.

---



### 4. Hover States

Hover interactions should be refined and minimal.

Examples:

- slight card lift
- subtle glow
- image scale
- underline transition
- CTA color shift

---



## Page-Level Guidance



### Home

Motion can be more expressive here.

Recommended:

- cinematic hero background
- subtle parallax
- layered section transitions

---



### Speakers

Motion should support browsing.

Recommended:

- card hover states
- smooth filter transitions
- subtle profile image movement

---



### Speaker Profile

Motion should feel calm and trust-building.

Recommended:

- soft section reveals
- sticky CTA behavior, if used
- gentle transitions between story and practical details

---



### Resources

Motion should stay functional and quiet.

Recommended:

- card hover states
- simple filtering transitions

---



### Booking Flow

Motion should reduce friction.

Recommended:

- smooth step transitions
- progress indicator animation
- gentle validation feedback

Avoid anything that makes the form feel unstable or overly animated.

---



## Accessibility

Respect reduced-motion preferences.

If a user has reduced motion enabled:

- disable parallax
- reduce background motion
- keep essential transitions simple

Motion should never block comprehension.
